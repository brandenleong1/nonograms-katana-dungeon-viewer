This site rebuilds a Nonograms Katana dungeon from its label (for example 3C-172582050), using the same rules the game uses, and draws it the way the game does. Click a ladder, hatch, portal, lever or gate to see the thing it connects to. The route panel suggests an order for clearing every objective.
The Solver plays the dungeon in the game's own combat engine and picks a tactic for every fight, using the fewest items first, then the least mental power, without dying. Robust mode works from the label alone and averages over many possible dice rolls; Exact mode needs the dice, from a save exported inside the dungeon (or a made-up seed to experiment), and gives a step-by-step script you can follow on the map with ← and →.
Monsters and people can depend on your quests and finds. The map assumes a new game until you import your save under Your hero (the game's Settings → Export file) or set your quests and bosses under Progress. The save is read in your browser only.
